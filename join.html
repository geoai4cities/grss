Join the chapter

All students and researchers of IISER Bhopal are welcome at our events. IEEE and GRSS membership adds journals, conference discounts, grants and a global network.

Why become a member

Research access

GRSS journals and magazine, IEEE Xplore discounts, and the GRSS Resource Center with tutorials and recorded lectures.

Conferences and grants

Reduced registration at IGARSS and InGARSS, student travel grants, student paper competitions and the Student Grand Challenge.

Community

A chapter of peers at IISER Bhopal, links to the GRSS MP Chapter and the student chapter at IIT Indore, and a worldwide society of 7,000+ members.

How to join, in four steps

  1. Join IEEE as a student member at ieee.org/membership/join-ieee, using your IISER Bhopal email and listing IISER Bhopal as your institution.
  2. Add the GRSS society during sign-up or later from your IEEE account, at grss-ieee.org/about/membership. The product code is MEMGRS029.
  3. Fill the chapter interest form so we can add you to the member list and tell you about events: forms.gle/39Rvyza5dLSFqYVU8.
  4. Join the WhatsApp group for announcements and meeting links: chat.whatsapp.com/BYW3iGHMtS69N46t8hBH7K.

Not ready to pay for membership? Fill the interest form and join the WhatsApp group anyway. Every event is open to non-members.
