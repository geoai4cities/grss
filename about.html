About

IEEE GRSS, and the student chapter that brings it to IISER Bhopal.

What is IEEE GRSS?

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organisation, with 533,000+ members in 190+ countries. The Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society (GRSS) is one of its 39 technical societies. Its mission is to advance the science, engineering, applications and education of remote sensing of the Earth, oceans, atmosphere and space, for the benefit of society. GRSS has 7,000+ members and 200+ chapters worldwide.

Fields of interest

  • Land and surface processes
  • Atmosphere and oceans
  • Cryosphere
  • Solid Earth and geodynamics
  • Natural disasters
  • Data processing techniques
  • Electromagnetics and radiative transfer
  • Sensors, platforms and instruments
  • Education, standards and policy

Conferences and journals

  • IGARSS, the flagship annual symposium
  • InGARSS, the India symposium
  • IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing
  • IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters
  • IEEE JSTARS
  • IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Magazine

Programs for students

  • Student Grand Challenge
  • Data Fusion Contest
  • Distinguished Lecturer program
  • GRSS summer schools
  • Travel grants for IGARSS and InGARSS
  • Student paper competitions

Vision

Build a vibrant community at IISER Bhopal that advances geoscience, remote sensing and geospatial AI through learning, research and collaboration.

Goals

  1. Bring experts to campus through a monthly lecture series.
  2. Build hands-on skills through workshops.
  3. Grow research culture through a paper-reading group and mentoring.
  4. Compete through a hackathon and the GRSS Student Grand Challenge.
  5. Connect members to IGARSS, InGARSS, travel grants, the GRSS MP Chapter and the student chapter at IIT Indore.
  6. Grow membership across Earth Sciences, EECS and Data Science and Engineering.

Where we fit

A student branch chapter has two parents: the geographic unit and the society.

IEEE side

IEEE → Region 10 (Asia-Pacific) → IEEE Madhya Pradesh Section → IEEE Student Branch, IISER Bhopal → GRSS Student Branch Chapter

Society side

IEEE GRSS → IEEE GRSS Madhya Pradesh Chapter (IIT Indore) → GRSS Student Branch Chapter, IISER Bhopal, with the GRSS student chapter at IIT Indore as a sister chapter.

How the chapter was formed

  1. 9 Jun 2026Petition submitted to IEEE by the founding student members, with Dr. Vaibhav Kumar as proposed Faculty Advisor.
  2. 23 Jul 2026Student Branch Counselor record updated; endorsements by the Counselor and the Faculty Advisor completed.
  3. 3 Aug 2026Chapter formally approved by IEEE after Region, Student Activities and Society President approvals.
  4. 23 Aug 2026First officer team elected: Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer and Webmaster.
  5. 21 Oct 2026Student Chapter Orientation, the first general meeting, with 34 participants.
  6. 24 Oct 2026Expert Talk 1 by Dr. Koyel Sur, Punjab Remote Sensing Centre, the first lecture of the Expert Talk Series.
